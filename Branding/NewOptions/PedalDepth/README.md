# Pedal depth studies

Three initial material studies generated with the built-in image_gen tool: amber enamel, teal aluminium and sculpted ember. These explore visible casing depth, metallic footswitches and richer lighting. Original transparent PNG outputs are preserved here. Full prompts are in prompts.md.

The user subsequently requested a degree of shading closer to the existing Preset Maestro gradient icon. These strong material studies remain references; the restrained revisions are in ../PedalGradients/.

Build-Comparison.ps1 preserves image proportions when rendering light/dark and 16/32/48-pixel previews. No production icon or app UI was changed. This study deliberately uses stronger material detail than the previously consulted Microsoft app-icon guidance suggests at small sizes; final production adaptation must simplify texture/reflections. Fine metal detail disappears at 16 pixels, while the arrow and circular switch remain the dominant shapes.
