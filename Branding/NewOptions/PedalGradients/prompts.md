## 1. Amber gradient

```text
Use case: style-transfer / logo-brand refinement.
Image 1: edit target, the current dimensional Pedal Send app icon. Image 2: STYLE REFERENCE ONLY, the existing Preset Maestro icon. Match the DEGREE AND RESTRAINT OF SHADING of Image 2; do not copy its guitar-pick silhouette, dial needle, markings, or logo design.
Primary request: bring the pedal closer to Preset Maestro's smooth, moderately shaded desktop-icon style. Reduce Image 1's photorealistic product rendering. Use one broad smooth colour gradient on the pedal face: amber yellow at the top gently shading through golden orange to warm copper orange at the bottom. The large right-facing forwarding arrow is charcoal, almost flat with just a very subtle bevel/contact shade, crisp and immediately readable. The circular footswitch has the same restrained grey-to-charcoal shading and slim light rim seen on Image 2's circular dial, but it remains a plain circular pedal switch with no pointer, ticks or needle.
Shading amount: restrained illustrated depth comparable to Image 2 at icon size. One gentle highlight near the upper-left edge, subtle shadow near lower-right, slim dark casing edge. No thick extruded enclosure or photographic studio reflections. Keep the gradient broad and smooth; no grain, brushed metal, machining, glitter, resin, glowing edges, flare, chrome streaks, luminous arrow or LED ring. A slightly satin look, clean forms and controlled depth. It should have more life than a flat vector symbol and much less gloss and physical detail than Image 1.
Preserve the Pedal Send design: vertical rounded rectangular pedal, two simple side connector tabs, large right arrow in upper half, one circular footswitch in lower half. Front-facing, no perspective tilt. The source's recognizable proportions and arrangement must remain. One square transparent PNG mark, centred, generous clear margin, no surrounding app tile, floor, external cast shadow, text, numbers, labels, watermark or additional controls. Genuine transparency. The goal is a moderately shaded graphic app logo rather than a product photograph.
```
